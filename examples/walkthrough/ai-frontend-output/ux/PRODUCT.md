# Product — Tidewatch

Tide and swell alerts for small-boat owners: pick a harbour, set the limits you care about, get a message when conditions cross them.

## Users and jobs
- Weekend boat owner: "Tell me when it is safe to launch, without me checking five sites."
- Kayak group organiser: "Warn the group when swell exceeds what we agreed."

## Core flows
1. **Discover -> sign up**: landing -> choose plan -> enter email -> confirm by magic link.
2. **Set an alert**: choose harbour -> choose limit (tide height, swell, wind) -> choose channel (email, SMS).
3. **Receive and act**: alert arrives -> open the harbour page -> see forecast and decision.

## Business rules
- Free plan: 1 harbour, email alerts. Paid plans add SMS and more harbours.
- Prices include tax; annual and monthly shown with the real yearly total; cancel from the account page in two clicks.
